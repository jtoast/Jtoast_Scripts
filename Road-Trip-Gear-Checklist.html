Solo · Sleeping in vehicle 2-3 nights/week · September departure · All 48 continental states

A Few Notes

Pack loose, not tight. Leave breathing room in bins for groceries picked up along the way — overpacking the car makes nightly sleep setup a chore.

Do a one-night practice run near home before the trip starts. Sleep in the car in your driveway or a nearby spot for a night — you'll immediately find what's missing or awkwardly placed before you're 800 miles from home.

Keep a "daily use" zone separate from "deep storage." Things you touch every day (food bins, sleep gear, toiletries) should be reachable without moving everything else.
